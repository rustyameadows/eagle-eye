Café des marais

The collector’s paper packet carries the name of the café beside the marsh. Each leaf is folded into fresh blotter with its field tag still attached.

Fine veins remain visible where the paper dries slowly. Scattered grains on the lower leaves identify the sandy track along the inlet.
