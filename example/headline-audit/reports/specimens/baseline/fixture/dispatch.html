Letter 17

The offshore office

The launch arrived before the first kettle had boiled. We took aboard the fresh charts, two letters, and a crate of cork floats with a cable tie around the middle.

There is a good working table now in the old signal room. From the window we can see the white line the boat leaves as she rounds the beacon.

Postmark

Beacon Point correspondence travelled with the morning stores during the summer watch.

Return by water

A second envelope went back with the empty crate. It carried readings the shore station had been waiting for since Tuesday.
