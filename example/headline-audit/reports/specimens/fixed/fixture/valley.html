Away from the ridge

From below, the upper bench looks continuous. Walking it reveals a string of small saddles separated by stony shelves. The survey follows those breaks to the last band of fir.

The second waypoint sits below a split boulder, the only reliable landmark when cloud settles on the ridge.

Bearing

Read the crest from west to east. Afternoon light makes the saddles easier to distinguish.

Alder flats

Cold air collects at the lowest station. The ground stayed frosted here for twenty minutes after the upper ridge warmed.
