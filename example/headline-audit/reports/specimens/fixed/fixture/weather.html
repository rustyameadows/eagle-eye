A weather station at the edge of the sound

Rain reaches the north windows first. The observer turns the wind vane a quarter turn by hand, waits for it to settle, and enters a fresh pressure reading.

Through the morning the notes move from high cirrus to a low grey ceiling. The barometer begins its climb before there is any break in the cloud.

Pressure series

Readings are corrected to sea level and taken from the same instrument throughout the shift.

Evening outlook

Loose cloud arrived behind the front. The final observer could see the far headland again from the west window.
