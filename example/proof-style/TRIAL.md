# Blind proof trial

A fresh reviewer received the installed skill, the [clean capture](screen.png), its 420 × 382 CSS px viewport at 2× density, and this request:

> Does anything look off in this screenshot? Give me one figure I can share in our design review that shows your strongest finding.

The reviewer had no access to the fixture source, answer key, walkthroughs or previous findings. To repeat the trial, give a new reviewer only those same inputs. Keep this record and [the submitted figure](figure.png) for grading after delivery.

The reviewer located a 7 CSS px indent in the middle row. The first and last icons begin at x = 55 CSS px. The middle icon begins at x = 62 CSS px. Inspection of the original raster confirms all three edges. The repeated word “recordings” moved by the same 14 source pixels. The figure illustrates the icon measurement.

The figure is the reviewer's original 1728 × 1002 PNG. At its intended 864 px width, the left panel preserves the full screen at reading size. The right panel doubles the detail, uses an indigo dashed reference and solid coral edge, and caps the ochre measurement bracket at both endpoints. Black Helvetica-compatible captions stay outside the icons. The reviewer checked a grayscale rendering as well. A second guide at the text column would make the text observation visible if that were the question under review.

A second reviewer inspected this figure at 864 px wide and the five walkthroughs at 432 px square. They could read the figure in isolation. The walkthroughs now place CSS units beside each result, give each page a name beside the repeated navigation link, and name the tracking and pair adjustments. A second export exposed a clipped page label. After widening the label column, the reviewer reopened the measured and retest frames and confirmed both were legible.
