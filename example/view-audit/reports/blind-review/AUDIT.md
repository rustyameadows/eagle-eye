Frozen findings from the supplied PNG packet. The first three issues affect keyboard navigation, identification of an imported record, and interpretation of an empty search. The smaller shifts below have reproducible visual references. All recommendations are open.

**Evidence convention.** W = wide, 1040 × 640 at 1×. T = tablet, 820 × 980 at 1×. C = compact, 390 × 720 at 2×. Each named view corresponds to `../blind-trial-corpus/{wide,tablet,compact}/<view>.png`. Coordinates below are CSS px from the original viewport origin. A solid edge is resolved to one source pixel. Ink comparisons use repeated glyphs where available. Allow one source pixel for antialiasing on a retest. Paired crops keep the same origin within each profile. [Same identifier at two widths](evidence/compact-import-id.png)
3. **F03 · Medium · The archived-search confirmation is hard to read.** W/T/C, `search-empty`. “Archived records were included” explains which records were searched, yet its solid ink is #A3B0A6 on white, about 2.25:1. All 77 supplied files match the index hashes and dimensions. The contact sheets cover every viewport. Higher-resolution pair comparisons cover the named components. The first pass established shell and reading order, the second compared sibling states, and the final pass remeasured suspects on the original PNGs and checked their profile boundaries. Individual glyphs outside the named comparisons received visual sampling.

**Legitimate differences.** Record includes an Inbox breadcrumb and the toolbar sits 30 px lower. The compact navigation becomes a horizontal strip. Compact tags wrap, bilingual instructions stack with Arabic right-aligned, and observations show fewer columns within the viewport. The Review panel depicts a centered conflict preview while Imports depicts a trailing progress drawer. Their different positions serve different tasks. The opposing card heights and different content lengths do not establish alignment defects.

**Open checks.** The register caption mentions ten readings and six are in view. The packet cannot establish internal scrolling or access to the remaining readings. Record’s notebook filename has visible truncation at tablet width. Access to its full text, content below the viewport, alternate locales, form submission, hover, other keyboard states and responsive widths between the three captures require live checks. The packet contains no design file, browser build, computed styles or post-fix frames. CSS causes and correction outcomes remain unverified.
