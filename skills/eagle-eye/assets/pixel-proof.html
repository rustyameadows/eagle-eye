Click a pixel or use the arrow keys. Hold Shift to move ten pixels. Scroll to pan. Grid lines appear at 8× and higher.

    Method and coordinates

    The reading-size view uses the capture’s device scale. The explorer uses native raster pixels. Its grid starts at the top left. Pixel values and change counts come from the original, opaque, 8-bit RGB samples, including partially covered antialiased edges.

    Any RGB-channel difference counts as changed. Darker and lighter describe the sign of 2126 ΔR + 7152 ΔG + 722 ΔB on encoded channels. A color can change while that weighted value stays equal. The exported measurements include the histogram of the largest absolute channel change per pixel, from 0 to 255.
